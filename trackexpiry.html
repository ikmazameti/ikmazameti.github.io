Privacy Policy

Expiry Tracker
Updated 22 October 2026

The short version

  • Your items stay on your phone. Names, dates, notes, prices and barcodes are stored only on your device. We have no account system and no server of our own, so we cannot see them.
  • No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Google services do receive some data: ads (AdMob), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), purchases (Google Play) and the barcode scanner (Google Play services). Details are below.
  • You are in control. Where the law requires it you are asked for consent for ads, and you can change that at any time under Settings → Privacy options. Buying Pro removes ads entirely.

1. Who we are

Expiry Tracker ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and published on Google Play by the developer listed on the App's store page. For any privacy question, write to mawulikazametibusiness@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored in a local database on your phone:

  • item and subscription names, expiry or renewal dates, notes, categories, prices and currency, reminder settings, scanned barcode numbers;
  • what you marked as used, thrown away or cancelled (your History);
  • app settings such as the reminder time, whether reminders are on, and simple counters used to decide when to show a tip (for example how many items you have saved).

We do not upload this information anywhere. If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may include the App's data in your device's own Google backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Reminders are created on your phone by the App itself. No reminder content is sent to us or to a push service.

3. Information processed by Google services

The App uses the following services, each provided by Google and governed by Google's own privacy terms (policies.google.com/privacy). The names of your items are never sent to them.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google AdMob (free version only) Advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions (views, clicks). To show banner, native, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Personalised ads are shown only where you have consented or where consent is not required.
Google User Messaging Platform Your consent choices and region. To ask for and remember your consent for ads where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and under some US state laws).
Google Analytics for Firebase App usage events (for example "an item was added", "an item was marked used", "the Pro screen was opened"), app instance ID, device model, OS version, country, and the advertising ID where available. To understand which features help people and to improve the App. Events never include what your items are called.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier. To find and fix crashes.
Google Play Billing Your purchase of Expiry Tracker Pro. Google handles payment; we receive only a purchase token and status, never your card details. To unlock Pro and restore it on reinstall.
Google code scanner (Google Play services) The camera feed while you scan, processed on your device by Google Play services. The App does not get camera access; it receives only the barcode number. To fill in an item from its barcode. The number is saved only on your device.
Google Play in-app review Handled entirely by Google Play. To occasionally offer a review prompt.

Analytics and crash reporting are switched off in our own development builds and run only in the version published on Google Play.

4. Ads, rewards and Pro

  • The free version shows ads. Full-screen ads are limited and never shown while you are typing, while you are buying Pro, or straight after you return from another app you opened from ours.
  • You may choose to watch a rewarded video in exchange for 24 hours without ads. Watching is always optional.
  • Expiry Tracker Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your expiry and renewal reminders. You are asked only after you save your first item, and you can turn reminders off in the App or in system settings.
  • Internet and network state: needed for ads, analytics, crash reports and purchases. Your items do not need the internet.

The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, location or microphone, and does not request camera access itself.

6. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Consent: personalised advertising and related cookies or identifiers, collected through the consent message.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reporting and aggregated usage statistics to keep the App working and improve it, and fraud prevention in advertising.
  • Contract: processing your Pro purchase.

7. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase event data is kept for up to 14 months; Crashlytics reports for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play keep data according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent at any time: Settings → Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
  • Delete any item, or all of them, from within the App. Uninstalling the App deletes its local data.
  • Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, to object, and to data portability, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no account data ourselves, most requests are best handled through your Google account and device settings; email us and we will help.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Security

Your items never leave your device through the App. Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep what we handle to the minimum the App needs.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the App's data practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App's release notes.
