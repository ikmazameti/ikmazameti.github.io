My Mango Manager: Farm Records

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

  • Without an account, your farm records stay on your phone. We don't see them.
  • We only receive data if you choose Back up (Pro) and sign in with Google.
  • We don't sell your data, don't show ads, and don't share your farm records with buyers, exporters or anyone else.
  • You can export your records or delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who we are

My Mango Manager ("the app") is made by 8Bits Technology, based in Accra, Ghana ("we", "us"). We decide how personal data in the app is used.

2. Two ways to use the app

Free, on this phone only

You can use the app without creating an account. Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device: blocks, sprays, harvests, sales, expenses, pest checks, photos and voice notes. We don't receive it. If you uninstall the app or lose the phone without a backup, that data is gone.

Pro, with Back up and team sharing

When you tap Back up and sign in with Google, a copy of your farm book is stored in the cloud. This lets you restore it on a new phone and share it with the people you invite. This section and the ones below describe what happens then.

4. Phone permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap "Take a photo of the bottle". Photos stay on your phone unless you back them up.
  • Microphone: only when you record a voice note.
  • Internet: only used for sign-in, Back up, subscriptions and (if you turn them on) crash reports. The rest of the app works offline.

You can refuse or turn off camera and microphone access in Android settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

5. Who else processes your data

We use these services to run Pro. Each handles data only to provide its service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Crashlytics): sign-in, cloud copy of records and photos, crash reports. Data may be stored on Google servers outside Ghana.
  • Google Play Billing: subscription payments, under Google's own privacy policy.

Inside your farm, everyone you invite can see the shared farm book. Your role settings decide who can add or delete records. We don't share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it. That includes buyers, exporters, input sellers, government agencies and data brokers.

6. How we protect it

  • Data travels between the app and our cloud services over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The cloud copy is protected by access rules: only members of your farm can read it.
  • On your phone, data sits in the app's private storage. Use a screen lock to protect it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Commission of Ghana as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Cloud data is kept while your account is active.
  • If Pro ends, your cloud copy stays for 12 months so you can renew without losing it. After that it's deleted, unless you renew or ask us to delete it sooner. Records on your phone are not affected.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your cloud data within 30 days. Copies in service backups expire within a further 60 days.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete my account and data → Delete. This removes your account and the farm's cloud copy if you're the owner. If you're a team member, it removes your access and your profile.

Without the app: email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete within 30 days.

Records on your phone are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To keep a copy first, use Settings → Export all data (CSV).

9. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and similar laws where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy
  • correct data that is wrong
  • delete your data, or stop using it
  • withdraw consent. For example, stop backing up, turn off crash reports, or sign out.

Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana or your local data protection authority.

10. Children

The app is made for farmers and farm businesses and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
