Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Last updated 8 November 2026 · Terms of use

The short version

This policy covers Mango Farm Records, the offline record book for mango growers everywhere, on Android (com.bofamsagrifield.mango) and iPhone.

  • Without an account, your farm records stay on your phone. We don't see them.
  • Your farm records only leave the phone after you sign in with Google (Pro). From then on they upload automatically whenever the phone is online, so nothing recorded in the field is lost.
  • The app sends anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics automatically (what went wrong, which screens are used; never your farm records) so we can fix problems and improve the app.
  • The app only reads your location when you tap Use this phone's location to save where your farm is. It never tracks you.
  • The optional photo and voice assistant reads photos and speech on your phone. Google's scanning tools send Google anonymous technical data, not your photos or words. You can switch the assistant off and type everything yourself.
  • We don't sell your data, don't show ads, and don't share your farm records with buyers, exporters or anyone else.
  • You can export your records at any time, and delete your account and data whenever you have one.

2. Two ways to use the app

Free, on this phone only

You can use the app without creating an account. Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device: blocks, sprays, harvests, sales, expenses, pest checks, photos and voice notes. We don't receive it. If you uninstall the app or lose the phone without a backup, that data is gone.

Photo and voice assistant (optional)

You can record things the usual way, with buttons and the phone's keyboard, or let the assistant help. Point the camera at a chemical bottle, receipt or weighing ticket, or say what you did, and the assistant fills in the form for you to check. Nothing is saved until you tap Yes, save.

  • Photos are read on your phone. Text and barcodes are recognised by Google's ML Kit tools running on the device. The photo and the text read from it are not sent to us or to Google. A photo taken only so the assistant can read it is deleted once it has been read. A photo you add to a record (for example a chemical bottle) is kept on your phone with that record.
  • Technical data from those tools: ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostic information, such as device and app details, how fast the tool ran, image size and error codes, so Google can keep the tools working. We don't receive this data.
  • Voice is turned into text on your phone where your phone supports it (most phones with Android 12 or newer). A short recording of what you said is kept with the record as a voice note.
  • Older phones: voice entry only works there if you turn on Use Google voice typing in Settings. Your speech is then sent to Google to be turned into text, under Google's privacy policy. This is off unless you turn it on.
  • To switch the assistant off: Settings → Photo and voice assistant → Off. The scanning tools then never run, and every record is entered with buttons.

Reminders

If you turn them on, the app shows reminders on your phone, such as "Block A: safe to pick today". They are made on the phone from your own records. Nothing is sent to us to make them, and you can switch each kind off in Settings → Reminders.

Pro, with Back up and sharing

When you sign in with Google, a copy of your farm book is stored in the cloud and kept up to date automatically. Everything you record is saved on the phone first and uploads by itself as soon as there's a connection. There's also a Sync now button. This lets you restore it on a new phone and share it with family or a helper you invite. This section and the ones below describe what happens then.

4. Phone permissions

  • Camera: only when you take a photo or scan a bottle, receipt, ticket, card or QR code. Photos stay on your phone unless you back them up.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice note or speak to the assistant.
  • Location (only when you tap "Use this phone's location"): asked the first time you tap it on the My farm screen. It is read only at that moment to save the farm's position, and it stays on your phone unless you sign in and back up. Never in the background, never tracked. You can type or paste the position instead.
  • Notifications: asked only when you tap Remind me (Android 13 and newer), to show your reminders. The app also restarts its morning reminder after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: used for sign-in, Back up and subscriptions, for crash reports and usage statistics, for automatic upload after you sign in, for Google voice typing (only if you turn it on), and by Google's scanning tools for anonymous diagnostics and model downloads. Recording works offline.

You can refuse or turn off camera, microphone and location access in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

5. Who else processes your data

We use these services to run the app and Pro. Each handles data only to provide its service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase): sign-in, cloud copy of records and photos, crash reports, usage statistics. Data may be stored on Google servers in other countries than yours. Google protects it under its own data processing terms, including the safeguards required for transfers out of the EU, UK and other regions.
  • Google Play Billing (and the App Store on iPhone): subscription payments, under their own privacy policies.
  • Google ML Kit (through Google Play services): on-device text and barcode recognition for the photo assistant. It sends Google anonymous diagnostics as described in section 2.
  • Google speech recognition: on-device on supported phones. It only sends audio to Google if you turn on Google voice typing.

Everyone you share your farm book with can see it. You decide who can add or delete records, and you can remove someone at any time. We don't share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it. That includes buyers, exporters, input sellers, government agencies and data brokers.

6. How we protect it

  • Data travels between the app and our cloud services over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The cloud copy is protected by access rules: only you and the people you share with can read it.
  • On your phone, data sits in the app's private storage. Use a screen lock to protect it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you, and the data protection authorities the law requires us to tell, without undue delay.

7. How long we keep it

  • Cloud data is kept while your account is active.
  • If Pro ends, your cloud copy stays for 12 months so you can renew without losing it. After that it's deleted, unless you renew or ask us to delete it sooner. Records on your phone are not affected.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your cloud data within 30 days. Copies in service backups expire within a further 60 days.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete my account and data → Delete. This removes your account and the farm's cloud copy if you're the owner. If someone shared their book with you, it removes your access and your profile.

Without the app: email mawulikazametibusiness@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete within 30 days.

Delete my account and data shows in Settings only while you are signed in, because only then is there an account and a cloud copy. Records on your phone are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To keep a copy first, use Export → Export all data (CSV), which works with or without an account.
